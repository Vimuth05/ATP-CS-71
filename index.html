Group dining

One table. One order. Fair bills.

Only the host scans the table QR. Friends join with a temporary invitation QR from the host's phone and choose their own food. Solo and one-phone ordering also work.

  • Shared meal selection for the whole table
  • Temporary invitation QR for guests
  • Flexible split payments
  • Order together or on your own
